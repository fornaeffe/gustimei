- Final Italian date arithmetic, declaration sufficiency, public service-date precision, and publication/expiry rules.
- Final pseudonym syntax, rename cadence, reserved/impersonation handling, and historical display behavior.
- Moderation grounds, severity levels, interim restriction threshold, second-review threshold, authority escalation, and measurable decision targets.
- DSA classification, anonymous-notice exception, owner/delegate authority evidence, internal/external redress wording, and transparency duties.
- Review, notice, identity, audit, and evidence retention periods, legal-claims hold criteria, and backup replay procedure.
- Production evidence encryption, malware scanning, format-aware metadata stripping, access monitoring, and deletion verification.
