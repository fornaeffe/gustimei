# Phase 3 evidence-informed interface principles

Date: 2026-08-16

Scope: a proportionate design review for the Phase 3 product shell, authentication, onboarding,
empty dashboard, and reusable ranking/review UI foundations. This is not a claim that interface
styling alone causes long-term wellbeing or retention.

## Selected principles

1. **Support autonomy and competence.** Self-determination research consistently associates autonomy
   and competence support with stronger intrinsic motivation. GustiMei therefore exposes one clear
   next step while preserving pause, back, skip, tie, undo, “not now,” and explicit-save paths.
   Progress and status feedback describe actual system state; the product has no streaks, artificial
   urgency, scarcity, points, or variable-reward loop.
2. **Use curiosity as an invitation, not a withholding tactic.** Experimental work found that
   epistemic curiosity can recruit reward-related processes and improve memory for answers. The
   landing page asks what a user's own comparisons may reveal and provides small, meaningful
   post-action reveals. It does not obscure essential information or manufacture suspense. Applying
   a learning experiment to product discovery is a tentative translation, not a proven engagement
   effect for GustiMei.
3. **Earn appropriately calibrated trust.** Systematic reviews identify transparency and interface
   quality as recurring antecedents of technology trust, while also showing that trust is contextual.
   GustiMei states what preference evidence does, separates private notes/rankings from public
   reviews, labels unverified review experience honestly, exposes recommendation limits, uses
   conventional controls, and avoids trust badges or popularity cues that do not prove reliability.
4. **Make purpose, status, and recovery perceivable.** W3C guidance supports clear headings and
   labels, consistent familiar controls, text error identification, visible focus, programmatically
   exposed status, alternatives to dragging, and adequate targets. Phase 3 uses semantic HTML, unique
   page titles, a skip link, persistent labels, live status/error messages, native dialogs, keyboard
   controls, practical 44px targets, strong focus indicators, reduced-motion behavior, and light/dark
   contrast tokens.
5. **Use calm aesthetics as support, not evidence.** Large first-impression studies associate visual
   complexity/colorfulness and category prototypicality with perceived appeal and trustworthiness,
   but aesthetic responses vary across people and cultures and do not prove actual usability.
   GustiMei uses restrained warm surfaces, a conventional editorial hierarchy, limited accent
   colors, stable non-photo fallbacks, and short non-essential motion. Usability and truthful content
   take precedence.

- Ryan, R. M., & Deci, E. L. (2000), [Intrinsic and Extrinsic Motivations: Classic Definitions and
  New Directions](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/04/2000_RyanDeci_IntExtDefs.pdf).

- Perski, O. et al. (2017), [Conceptualising engagement with digital behaviour change interventions:
  a systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1007/s13142-016-0453-1). Its intervention context limits
